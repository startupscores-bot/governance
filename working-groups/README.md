# CAIPE Working Groups

Working groups organize collaboration within a defined scope delegated by the
Steering Committee. Participation is open to anyone who follows the Code of
Conduct.

## Active working groups

No CAIPE working groups have been chartered yet.

## Creating a working group

Open a governance proposal containing:

- name, mission, scope, and explicit out-of-scope areas;
- deliverables and success measures;
- authority requested from the Steering Committee;
- at least two proposed leads, preferably from different employers;
- communication channels, meeting cadence, and public record location;
- dependencies on repositories or other groups; and
- an initial term or review date.

The Steering Committee approves a working group by simple majority after at
least seven calendar days of public review.

## Operating requirements

Each group must:

- maintain a charter in this directory;
- hold open meetings when meetings are needed;
- publish agendas, minutes, decisions, attendance, and action items;
- report status at least quarterly;
- keep repository ownership and decision boundaries current; and
- escalate cross-project or unresolved governance issues to the Steering
  Committee.

Working-group decisions use consensus unless the charter defines a voting
process. A group may not change project-wide policy, licensing, trademarks, or
governance unless the Steering Committee explicitly delegates that authority.

## Review and dissolution

The Steering Committee reviews each group at its chartered review date or at
least annually. A group may be renewed, revised, merged, paused, or dissolved.
On dissolution, the group archives its records and identifies the new owner of
unfinished work and maintained assets.
