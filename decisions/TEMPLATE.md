# CAIPE Decision YYYY-NNN: Short title

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** accepted | rejected | superseded
- **Decision owner:** @github-handle
- **Authority:** Steering Committee | Maintainers | Other documented body
- **Scope:** affected repositories, assets, or policies
- **Proposal:** link
- **Implementation:** links, or `Not applicable`
- **Supersedes:** decision ID, or `None`
- **Superseded by:** decision ID, or `None`

## Motion

State the exact decision that was considered.

## Context

Summarize the problem, constraints, and relevant evidence. Link detailed
discussion instead of duplicating it.

## Alternatives considered

- Alternative and why it was not selected.

## Decision and rationale

Record the outcome and the reasons that determined it.

## Review and vote

- **Review window:** YYYY-MM-DD through YYYY-MM-DD
- **Quorum:** requirement and result
- **Votes:** named votes or link to the authoritative tally
- **Abstentions:** names, or `None`
- **Recusals:** names and public reason when appropriate, or `None`
- **Result:** passed | failed

Do not include confidential personal, conduct, legal, or security information.

## Impact and follow-up

- Owners, deadlines, implementation work, compatibility impact, and rollback
  plan.
