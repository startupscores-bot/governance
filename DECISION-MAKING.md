# Decision Making

## Default: consensus

CAIPE seeks rough consensus: concerns are heard and addressed, but unanimity is
not required. The decision owner summarizes the outcome and rationale in the
relevant issue or pull request.

## Routine decisions

Repository maintainers and working-group leads may make routine decisions
within their documented scope through normal review. A minimum of one approving
maintainer is required unless a repository defines a stronger rule.

Minor, reversible changes may use lazy consensus after three business days.

## Steering Committee proposals

Steering Committee proposals use a public GitHub issue or pull request and a
minimum seven-calendar-day review period. The proposer must state:

- the decision requested;
- scope and affected projects;
- alternatives considered;
- operational, community, and financial impact; and
- implementation and rollback plan, when applicable.

The Chair may shorten the review period for a documented security, legal, or
operational emergency. The committee must review the emergency decision within
14 days.

## Votes

Quorum is a majority of seated, non-recused members.

| Decision | Threshold |
| --- | --- |
| Routine committee decision | Simple majority of votes cast at quorum |
| Charter or governance amendment | Two-thirds of all seated members |
| Project admission, archival, or transfer | Two-thirds of all seated members |
| License, trademark, fiscal host, or foundation change | Two-thirds of all seated members |
| Steering Committee member removal | Two-thirds of all other seated members |

Abstentions do not count as votes cast but do count toward quorum unless caused
by recusal. A tied vote does not pass.

Each formal vote record includes the motion, review window, individual votes,
abstentions, recusals, result, and effective date.

## Conflicts of interest

Participants disclose financial, employment, personal, or organizational
interests that could reasonably affect their judgment. A recused person may
provide factual context but must not vote or use private influence on the
decision.

Recusals are recorded publicly unless doing so would expose confidential
personal information.

## Appeals

1. Technical decisions are first reconsidered by the relevant maintainers or
   working group.
2. A contributor may appeal an unresolved process or cross-project issue to the
   Steering Committee with a written summary.
3. The Steering Committee decides whether to hear the appeal and publishes its
   rationale.
4. Code of Conduct appeals follow the confidential enforcement process rather
   than this public process.
