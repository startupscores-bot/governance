# CAIPE Code of Conduct

CAIPE adopts the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version
2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in CAIPE a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- demonstrating empathy and kindness;
- respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- accepting responsibility and learning from mistakes; and
- focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, sexualized attention,
trolling, sustained disruption, publication of private information, and other
conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in project spaces and when an individual officially represents
CAIPE in public spaces. Project spaces include repositories, issue trackers,
meetings, chat channels, events, and other community forums.

## Reporting and enforcement

Before CAIPE governance becomes operational, the initial Steering Committee
must publish and test a private reporting channel here.

Do not report conduct incidents in a public issue.

The security reporting address is only for product and infrastructure
vulnerabilities. It is not the Code of Conduct reporting channel. Questions,
governance proposals, feature requests, and ordinary bugs should remain in
public discussions or issues.

The enforcement team will:

- acknowledge reports promptly;
- protect reporter and witness privacy;
- investigate fairly and without conflicts of interest;
- apply proportionate corrective action; and
- provide an appeal path to uninvolved reviewers.

Committee members named in a report or sharing a material conflict must recuse
themselves. Enforcement actions may include correction, warning, temporary
restriction, suspension, or permanent removal from project spaces.
