# CAIPE Steering Committee

## Status

The committee becomes active when at least three members are seated. No members
are seated yet. The roster will be maintained in this file through public pull
requests.

## Composition

- Five voting seats.
- Members serve as individuals, not as employer representatives.
- No more than two voting members may share the same employer or controlling
  organization.
- Members serve one-year terms and may serve at most three consecutive terms.
- A Chair and Secretary are elected annually by the committee from among its
  members. These roles do not receive additional votes.

If an affiliation change violates the concentration limit, affected members
have 30 days to resolve it. Otherwise, the member with the shortest remaining
term steps down; ties are resolved by committee vote with affected members
recused.

## Initial formation

Initial committee membership will be defined later through a separate public
CAIPE governance proposal.

- No current role, repository permission, or organizational affiliation grants
  automatic membership.
- The proposal must identify each candidate, affiliation, term, selection
  method, and effective date.
- Each candidate must publicly accept the charter, Code of Conduct, and conflict
  disclosure requirements.
- The five-seat and affiliation-limit rules apply to the initial roster.
- The initial committee serves no longer than six months before the first
  regular election.

Before at least three members are seated, the committee has no quorum and may
not make binding decisions.

## Regular elections

### Eligibility

- Any contributor may nominate themselves or another contributor.
- A nominee must accept publicly, disclose current employer affiliation, and
  describe their contributions and priorities.
- The election record date and voter roll are published before voting opens.
- Eligible voters are CAIPE organization members and repository maintainers on
  the record date.

### Process

1. Nominations remain open for at least 14 calendar days.
2. Candidate statements remain public for at least seven calendar days before
   voting closes.
3. Each eligible voter may approve up to the number of open seats.
4. Candidates are ranked by approval count, subject to the affiliation limit.
5. Ties are resolved by a public runoff.
6. The Secretary publishes the result, voter roll, tally, recusals, and term
   dates in this repository.

The committee may use a private ballot service when necessary to protect voters,
but must publish the method and aggregate tally.

## Vacancies and removal

- A vacancy is filled by special election unless fewer than 90 days remain in
  the term.
- The committee may appoint an interim member until the special election, with
  approval from two-thirds of all remaining members.
- A member may resign at any time through a public pull request.
- A member may be removed for sustained inactivity, undisclosed conflicts,
  Code of Conduct violations, or serious breach of duty by a two-thirds vote of
  all other seated members.
- Removal proposals receive at least 14 calendar days for response unless a
  confidential or urgent safety matter requires temporary suspension.

## Member responsibilities

Members must:

- attend and prepare for meetings;
- review governance proposals promptly;
- act in the project's interest;
- disclose affiliations and relevant conflicts;
- recuse themselves when impartiality is reasonably in doubt;
- protect confidential conduct and security information; and
- maintain public decision and meeting records.

Three consecutive missed meetings without notice trigger an inactivity review.

## Meetings

- Regular meetings occur at least monthly.
- The agenda is published at least 48 hours in advance.
- Minutes, attendance, decisions, and action items are published within seven
  days.
- Meetings are open by default. Closed sessions are limited to conduct,
  security, legal, or personnel matters, and the public minutes must record the
  reason for closure.
